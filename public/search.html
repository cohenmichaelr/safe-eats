What “food type” means here, and why there is no state filter

Food type reads the name, not a record. The state's licence extract has no column describing what an establishment serves — so choosing Greek finds places whose licensed name contains Greek, gyro, souvlaki or taverna. That is a fact about the licence. It is not a statement that the kitchen serves Greek food, and a place that matches nothing is not a place that serves nothing.

Roughly 7 in 10 establishments match no food type at all, because they are named after a person, a street or a mood. Use it to narrow a search, never to conclude a kind of restaurant is absent from a town.

State — these are Florida's own licensing records, so every establishment here is in Florida. There is nothing to narrow.

Everything listed is a permanent food service establishment; mobile vendors, caterers, temporary events and vending machines are excluded because they are not premises you can walk into.
